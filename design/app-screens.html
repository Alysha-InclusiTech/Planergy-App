Planergy app screens

Five phone screens at 390 × 844, all working from one shared plan: change something on one screen and the others update. The notes under each one name the Flutter widgets to build it with.

9:41

Onboarding

Three check-in questions. Each answer says what it changes, so the choice means something.

  • Energy as three ChoiceChip-like cards in a Row of Expanded, showing the minutes each allows
  • Work hours and talking as SegmentedButton; "Flexible" reveals a showTimePicker start time
  • Primary button pinned in Scaffold.bottomNavigationBar or a SafeArea footer
9:41

Add tasks

A brain dump. Speak or type; every line becomes a task with a guessed time and effort you can see straight away.

  • Multi-line TextField (maxLines: null) beside a 56px round mic button; hide the mic on non-verbal days
  • Voice via the speech_to_text package, split on new lines and "and"
  • "Just added" list of ListTiles with time and effort pips; swipe to delete with Dismissible
9:41

Review

Important tasks are placed first, so the screen splits into the two groups and shows live how much of today's energy they fill.

  • Budget card on top: LinearProgressIndicator or a Row of segments sized by minutes
  • Two sections; the star IconButton moves a task between them
  • Tap the time or effort to open a showModalBottomSheet with the presets
9:41

Day and week

Day: the timeline, each block as long as the task. Week: the next seven days, with what didn't fit today rolled forward.

  • Day/Week SegmentedButton switching two views
  • Day timeline as a Stack of Positioned blocks over hour rows; LongPressDraggable + DragTarget to move
  • Week as a ListView of day cards, each with its own budget bar
9:41

Dopamine menu

Small rewards, grouped by length. Each row says whether it still fits today before you add it.

  • Add-your-own TextField with a trailing add button at the top
  • Grouped ListView; the trailing "Add" becomes "Added" for a moment, then the row shows its time
  • Long-press a reward to edit or delete it (showModalBottomSheet)
